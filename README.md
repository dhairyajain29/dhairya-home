# Dhairya Residence

Complete house gallery with 17 colour renders, 33 model views, a 2-minute Full HD 60 fps film with 17 chapters made from the latest realistic renders, 63 service points and three annotated service sheets, an interactive 3D viewer and a portable GLB model. The film uses gentle zooms and transitions through the realistic images; it is not a continuous 3D walkthrough. The separate interactive viewer retains the dimensioned model and its live tour.

The facade follows the supplied original elevation, with a silver-grey horizontal gate fitted to the confirmed 7-foot-4-inch opening and 8-inch pier. The rear 2-foot chhaja faces the terrace within the house boundary; the rear under-stair laundry includes clothes storage.
