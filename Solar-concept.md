# Dhairya Residence — 3 kW solar visualization

The model shows six nominal 500 W modules, totalling 3 kWp, above the revised rear mumty room and washroom. Four panels are landscape and two portrait. Module bodies retain the previously selected visualization envelope of 1800 ×1134 ×30 mm.

The rear roof top remains +33′11″ above the road. The new 2′ exposed-side chhaja widens the roof outline while all room dimensions stay unchanged. Panels remain above the original room/washroom roof region; the chhaja has not been assumed to have verified capacity for solar loads. The original 3′ ×4′4″ duct aperture has a raised ventilated glass cover.

This is a spatial concept. Panel tilt, compass orientation, supports, anchors, maintenance access, drainage, inverter, battery and wiring are not installation specifications. The narrow washroom-roof area still needs a coordinated mounting layout. The original supporting roof is approximately 190.8 sq ft; the enlarged opaque roof including the chhaja is approximately 316.1 sq ft, with the full duct aperture excluded. Roof area alone does not establish a workable installation.

A 3 kW rating does not establish that the whole house can be powered whenever required. Consumption, sunlight, simultaneous appliance use and the grid/storage arrangement need to be considered. No energy-yield, battery-size or outage-backup guarantee is made by this visualization.

The unresolved inputs are monthly electricity units, summer AC/geyser usage, location/orientation/shading, and the appliances and hours for which backup is wanted.
