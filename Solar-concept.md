# Dhairya Residence — 3 kW solar visualization

The model shows six nominal 500 W modules, totalling 3 kWp, above the revised rear mumty room and washroom. Four panels are landscape and two portrait. Module bodies retain the previously selected visualization envelope of 1800 × 1134 × 30 mm.

The rear roof top remains +33′11″ above the road. The rear 2′ chhaja projects toward the open terrace to the marked dotted line, across the left passage and washroom frontage. The roof also covers the left passage beside the washroom. Side and rear roof edges stay within the original 15′ × 40′ house boundary, and all room dimensions stay unchanged. Panels remain above the original room/washroom roof region; the chhaja has not been assumed to have verified capacity for solar loads. The original 3′ × 4′4″ duct aperture has a raised ventilated glass cover.

This is a spatial concept. Panel tilt, compass orientation, supports, anchors, maintenance access, drainage, inverter, battery and wiring are not installation specifications. The narrow washroom-roof area still needs a coordinated mounting layout. The original supporting roof is approximately 190.8 sq ft; the revised opaque roof, including the passage cover and terrace-facing chhaja, is approximately 232.8 sq ft, with the full duct aperture excluded. Roof area alone does not establish a workable installation.

A 3 kW rating does not establish that the whole house can be powered whenever required. Consumption, sunlight, simultaneous appliance use and the grid/storage arrangement need to be considered. No energy-yield, battery-size or outage-backup guarantee is made by this visualization.

The unresolved inputs are monthly electricity units, summer AC/geyser usage, location/orientation/shading, and the appliances and hours for which backup is wanted.
