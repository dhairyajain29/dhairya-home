01 / MUMTY LAYOUT

A room at the rear.

A 14′4″ × 9′8″ bedroom and 7′0″ × 4′4″ washroom follow your new plan. The entrance stays at the marked left side. The terrace, side approach and open-to-sky shaft remain open.

02 / HEIGHTS & LOFT

Your inside heights.

11′6″ clear in the front tower and 10′6″ clear in the rear room and washroom. The ground bathroom has a storage loft over its whole area, with the underside at 7′6″. Loft thickness is unspecified.

03 / ROOFTOP SOLAR

A 3 kW proposal.

Six nominal 500 W modules sit over the rear room and washroom. Mounting, access and roof-edge clearances need a final site layout. Whether 3 kW meets your household needs depends on use, sunlight and the chosen system.
