DHAIRYA RESIDENCE — REVISED HOME VISUALIZATION

START HERE
Open Open-house-gallery.html in a current browser. Keep all files and subfolders together.
This package uses local files and works offline; no internet connection or installation is needed for the gallery.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Open-house-gallery.html: responsive gallery, image enlargements, filters and video chapters.
- Complete-house-walkthrough.mp4: 2 minutes 54 seconds, 1920 x 1080 Full HD, 24 frames per second, silent.
- Photoreal-renders/: 12 PNG images illustrating proposed finished colours and materials.
- Images/: 25 PNG views from the revised dimensioned 3D model.
- Interactive-3D-viewer.html: interactive house model. Open in a current browser with 3D graphics enabled.
- Dhairya-house-model.glb: portable 3D model for compatible 3D software; units are metres.
- Revision-notes.md: confirmed dimensions and changes, and the remaining assumptions.
- Solar-concept.md: proposed 3 kW solar layout, roof fit and household power considerations.
- Third-party-license.txt: relevant software licence.

VIEWING AND SAVING
Select any gallery image to enlarge it; use the arrows to browse and Save image to keep a copy.
Press Escape or select the close button to leave the enlarged view.
Use the floor filters to browse the colour images.
Play the video in the gallery, or open the MP4 directly in your video player.
Select any of the 25 time chapters to jump to that space.
If your browser cannot play the MP4, open it in a compatible video player.
The notes are plain text in Markdown format and can also be read in a text editor.

THE CONFIRMED REVISION
Mumty rear bedroom: 14'4" x 9'8"; washroom: 7'0" x 4'4".
Front tower clear inside height: 11'6"; rear room/washroom clear inside height: 10'6".
Ground bathroom: full-area loft underside at 7'6"; thickness was not provided.
Ground bathroom: wall-mounted WC, shower and diverter.
First-floor front bathroom: Indian toilet, shower/diverter and geyser.
First-floor rear bathroom: wall-mounted WC, diverter and geyser.
Mumty washroom: Indian toilet and diverter.
Solar: six nominal 500 W modules, totalling 3 kW, above the rear room and washroom.

HOW TO READ THE VISUALS
Photo-style renders are proposed finishes and may differ in small details from the model.
The model and video use the confirmed plan dimensions and revisions. Remaining inferred opening offsets and other assumptions are described in Revision-notes.md.
The imagery is not an as-built survey and does not certify every constructed detail.
Solar mounting is illustrative. A 3 kW array does not guarantee continuous whole-house power or outage backup; see Solar-concept.md.
